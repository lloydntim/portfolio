CASE STUDY

AnzaKen — connecting farmers to markets

An agritech platform giving smallholder farmers direct access to buyers and financing — built from the mobile app down to the data pipeline behind it.

ROLE
Full-stack & product engineer
TIMELINE
8 months
STACK
React Native, Node.js, PostgreSQL
[ cover image — AnzaKen app ]

Smallholder farmers across East Africa often sell through several layers of middlemen before produce reaches a buyer, losing margin and visibility at every step. AnzaKen set out to close that gap with a direct, mobile-first marketplace.

THE CHALLENGE

Trust, connectivity and cash flow

Farmers needed a way to list produce and get paid reliably, often on low-end Android devices with unreliable connectivity. Buyers needed confidence that listings were accurate and that payment and delivery would actually happen. Any solution had to work offline-first and settle payments without requiring either side to hold a bank account.

On the business side, financing partners needed transaction data to underwrite short-term credit to farmers — which meant the platform had to produce clean, trustworthy records from day one.

[ screenshot — listing flow ]
THE APPROACH

Offline-first, API-driven, built to scale

I built the mobile app in React Native with a local-first data layer that queues listings and orders when offline and syncs once connectivity returns. The backend is a Node.js API on PostgreSQL, with a separate service handling payment reconciliation and generating the transaction history financing partners rely on.

I also built the analytics dashboard the operations team uses to monitor listings, flag anomalies and track farmer onboarding across regions.

"The offline-first design meant farmers in low-connectivity regions could use the app exactly like everyone else — the sync just happened when it could."

THE OUTCOME

A platform ready for financing partners

AnzaKen launched across three regions with a transaction history clean enough for its first financing partner to begin underwriting farmer credit directly from platform data.
